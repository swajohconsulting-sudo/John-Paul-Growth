Because these services involve time, expertise, and creative work, refund eligibility is limited as described below.

3. Refund Eligibility

Non-refundable circumstances

Payments are generally non-refundable once:

  • Work has started

This reflects the time-based and creative nature of the services.

A partial refund may be considered only if:

  • A written cancellation request is received before any work begins, and

Approval of any refund remains at my sole discretion.
